01. About

      

        

          
Hi, I'm Vansh — a final-year B.Tech Computer Science student at VIT-AP University in Amaravati, specializing in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning and graduating in 2027.

          
I started working on real ML problems early. In my first year I did two internships in Bangalore: at Fox Trading I built a model that predicts parking availability from historical occupancy data, and at Personifwy I trained a 500M-parameter language model end to end — data preparation, tokenization, GPU training and checkpoint evaluation. Those internships pulled me toward the engineering side of AI: how models are trained, served and kept secure.

          
Most of what I learn, I learn by rebuilding it from scratch — a spiking neural network in NumPy, an LLM inference engine with its own KV cache, a GRPO trainer without the RL libraries. It's slower than calling a library, but it's the fastest way I know to understand why things work.

          
Right now I'm interested in three things: making LLMs fast to serve, making them reason better with reinforcement learning, and making the APIs that host them harder to steal from. That last one is turning into a research paper.

    Detecting Model-Extraction Probing with Semantic Embeddings and MMD
    
            
    An inference-API wrapper that spots attackers trying to clone or invert an LLM by watching the shape of their query traffic — comparing the distribution of semantic query embeddings against benign traffic with maximum mean discrepancy.
    
            
      
              
    • Calibrated on benign traffic only — no attack data needed to set thresholds.
      • 
              
    • Session-level risk profile with a five-level graduated response, to avoid punishing legitimate users.
      • 
              
    • Benchmarked against PRADA, SEAT and other baselines on a 14-pair extraction benchmark.
